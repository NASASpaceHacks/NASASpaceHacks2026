First steps on Mars, without Earth in the loop.

Marswalk plans a surface walk on real NASA terrain, checks it against 12 cited NASA EVA constraints, and guides the astronaut through an AR navigator, even when the link to the habitat drops.

Mount Sharp, where Curiosity is driving.

A 5 × 5 km study area on Curiosity's 2025–26 boxwork traverse at 4.805°S 137.388°E, the challenge's own example.

02 · The plan

Planned in the habitat, on 1-metre terrain.

From Hab-1, no science stop passes all 12 constraints. Staged from the PR-1 rover, it's a GO. Every plan is re-walked in a simulator in about 38 ms.

03 · On foot

The AR navigator brings her home.

Chevrons on the ground and one instruction at a time. Mars has no GPS, so it dead-reckons between landmark fixes. If the link to the habitat drops, it goes autonomous within about 3 seconds.
