12 constraints

Twelve limits, each one cited.

Drawn from public NASA EVA documents. Nine come straight from a source. Three are our own design parameters, labelled as such.

  • 01
    20°

    Maximum slope

    Suited crew never plan a walk steeper than 20°, up, down or across.

    xEVA ConOps EVA-EXP-0042 Rev B · NTRS 20230005904
  • 02
    6 h

    EVA duration

    A nominal spacewalk is 6 hours, start to finish.

    xEVA ConOps EVA-EXP-0042 Rev B
  • 03
    30 min

    Egress and ingress

    Half an hour in the airlock each way, counted inside the 6 hours.

    xEVA ConOps EVA-EXP-0042 Rev B
  • 04
    60 min

    Contingency oxygen

    A backup hour of oxygen that a plan never spends.

    xEVA ConOps EVA-EXP-0042 Rev B
  • 05Design parameter
    0.5×

    Walk-back at half speed

    A crew member must always be able to walk home at half speed.

    Team assumption · Bellcomm TR-69-340-4
  • 06
    30 min

    Shelter in reach

    Shelter from a solar storm is never more than 30 minutes away.

    DRA 5.0 Addendum SP-2009-566-ADD · arXiv:2502.02469
  • 07Design parameter
    ≤10 min

    Radio gaps

    No more than 10 minutes out of radio contact with the habitat or rover.

    Caswell et al., ASCEND 2025 · NTRS 20250006063
  • 08Design parameter
    5°

    Daylight

    Sun at least 5° above the horizon. No night EVAs.

    DRA 5.0 Addendum SP-2009-566-ADD
  • 09
    352 W

    Suit heat

    The suit's 30-minute average heat load stays within 352 W. Rests are added when it doesn't.

    ICES-2026-127 · NTRS 20130014146
  • 10
    2 km

    Walking radius

    Never farther than 2 km from the habitat on foot.

    NTRS 20230008183 · xEVA ConOps
  • 11
    1 km

    Plume keep-out

    Stay 1 km clear of landing pads, where plume ejecta lands.

    Hoffman et al., NTRS 20160002370
  • 12Soft
    16.7°

    Slope comfort

    A softer limit where suited metabolic data ends. Steeper is flagged, not forbidden.

    Norcross et al., NASA/TP-2010-216125

Every plan is re-walked in a simulator against all hard constraints before it is shown. And "stay in" is always a valid answer.
