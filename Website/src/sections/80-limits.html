What we checked, and what we didn’t.

We say “calibrated” and “reproduces”. We do not say “validated”, and nothing here has been tested with suited people. Each check sits next to its limit.

Other limits.

  • Simulation only. No field test and no suited-human test.
  • Our assumptions, labelled in the app: mapping thermal inertia to walking effort, the dose constants, and the rover radio relay.
  • Science values are ours. The 12 stops are real, cited Curiosity targets, but their 0–100 science values are our team’s scoring, not NASA rankings.
  • REMS reading is the latest outreach-feed value, not the scenario sol. Sol 412 predates the feed, so the season falls back to an assumed Ls 240.
  • The AR navigator is a phone app, not suit hardware, and the link is local Wi-Fi. A phone stands in for the helmet display.
  • The 12 constraints are drawn from public NASA EVA documents, each cited. They are not NASA flight rules, and nothing here says a route is safe for crew.
  • No NASA relationship, and no claim of hours of planning saved: there is no published baseline.
