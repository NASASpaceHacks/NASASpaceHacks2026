01 · The problem

Earth is 22 light-minutes away.

On Mars nobody can phone mission control mid-walk. The signal takes minutes each way, and for about two weeks around solar conjunction it cannot get through at all.

EarthSignal at the speed of lightMars
One-way light time12.5MIN
Ask, then wait for the answer25.0MIN
Round trip6–44 min

Every question to Earth costs a coffee break, or most of an hour.

Solar conjunction~2 weeks

With the Sun in between, Mars cannot be reached. Blackout.

The consequenceSo the crew plans its own walks.

The planner has to live on Mars, next to the habitat.

“The crew will likely be more involved in planning their EVAs than as is currently done on ISS… crew input, ground based planners, and automated planning tools.”
