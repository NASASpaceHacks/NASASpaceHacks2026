02 · The system

From habitat to helmet.

Three pieces, one chain. The plan is made and checked in the habitat, sent as a tiny signed file, and followed on the astronaut's own display.

01
Habitat console

Plan it like a map.

The EVA officer picks a start (the habitat or the pressurized rover) and the places to visit, Google-Maps style. Marswalk answers GO or NO-GO, with the reason and the exact stretch where it fails.

  • 1 m terrain model of Gale crater
  • Ways forward: closest safe point, move rover, start from rover
  • Timeline with latest safe return

02
Signed route

Small enough for any link.

The verified plan leaves the habitat as one cryptographically signed file, over the local surface link. Everything runs on the habitat server. No internet needed.

  • Ed25519 signature on every route
  • Local surface link only
  • Habitat-side server, offline

03
AR navigator

Follow the chevrons.

A phone stands in for the helmet display. The camera fills the screen; chevrons on the ground bend toward the next steer point. One instruction at a time. If the link drops, it keeps guiding, on its own.

  • Dead-reckons (Mars has no GPS), fixes every ~10 min
  • >25 m off route: local A* reroute
  • Link lost: autonomous within ~3 s
  • Voice commands
