Use of AI
  • The team chose the problem, the concept, the design, the data and the scope, and reviewed the work.
  • Most of the code was written with Anthropic’s Claude Code, under the team’s direction.
  • AI is not in the safety path. No AI decides a route; the verdict comes from the engine and its simulator.
  • Blind AI reviewers were used once as a consistency check of the engine’s verdicts (V4). That shows the logic is consistent, not that the models are right.
  • No AI-generated imagery or video.
