## Citation gaps and interpretation

- The original report contains opaque `turn...` citation/image markers that cannot be resolved from this directory. Explicit URLs and DOI links are included; marker-only references remain unrecoverable.
- Incomplete bibliographic fields are left incomplete rather than invented. This is a local-source compilation, not an independently verified publication-ready bibliography.
- Rover studies and reduced-gravity/Earth experiments do not validate the prototype's human Mars walking assumptions. The local notes identify its speed, slope cutoff, directional coefficients and time/reserve budgets as engineering assumptions.
- Historical access results: [HTTP checks](../docs/research/evidence/http_checks.json). Actual elevation crop provenance: [jezero_dem.json](../examples/jezero_dem.json). The compilation date is not a new external access date.
